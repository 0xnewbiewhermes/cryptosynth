## CryptoSynth Editorial Rules

These instructions apply to every new or revised file under `src/content/blog/`.

Before writing or editing an article, read and follow `.claude/skills/cryptosynth-write.md`. Its **Naturalness Gate** and **Human Edit Pass** have priority over rigid templates, article length targets, and SEO padding.

Mandatory editorial behavior:

- Write in natural Indonesian unless the user explicitly requests another language.
- Keep common crypto and technical terms in English when that is how Indonesian readers normally use them.
- Sound personal, direct, skeptical, and practical - like explaining research to a friend.
- Separate verified facts, the author's opinion, and speculation.
- Prefer primary sources and verify every time-sensitive number or claim.
- Never fabricate first-hand usage, transactions, screenshots, results, quotes, or personal experience.
- Do not force identical section order across articles.
- Avoid repeated disclaimers, summary repetition, symmetrical sections, generic hooks, generic conclusions, and unnecessary tables.
- Cut filler. Every paragraph must add a fact, instruction, context, or a reasoned opinion.
- Run the Human Edit Pass before publishing.
- Preserve existing frontmatter, slug, links, images, and factual caveats unless the requested edit requires a change.
- Do not publish automatically generated prose without a final naturalness review.

A draft is not ready when it merely sounds polished. It is ready when it sounds specific to CryptoSynth and could not be reused unchanged for another crypto project.
